**Synopsis**

The protagonist, Nozomi Anesaki (first name can be changed), has been enjoying a fulfilling school life. A year and a half has passed since she entered a prestigious private high school.

Cheerful and polite, she is admired by everyone at school. She maintains excellent grades, actively participates in the student council, serves as class representative, and even volunteers in her spare time. Thanks to her efforts, she has earned the respect of both teachers and students. However, she has a secret she can't tell anyone... (though it's really nothing serious).

Her perpetually grumpy and cheeky younger brother, the student council president she admires, and a mysterious transfer student who suddenly appears out of nowhere...

Surrounded by these unique characters, another exciting day in her fulfilling high school life begins!
(taken from Steam description)

**Translation notes**

This is my first translation project of this size. Before this, I’d only translated manga, and even that was into another language. So perhaps I bit off more than I could chew this time. But hopefully it's still decent. I posted the full English script on my tumblr. Sorry, but this is the only alternative I can offer to those who want to play on other platforms.

Some honorifics were translated where appropriate, while others were left unchanged. Details:

-sama: in the game, the honorific is only used when referring to Wald, so I translated it as "Lord Wald". It's pretty fitting since he's a prince.

-aneki: this is the way Shun refers to mc. It's a somewhat rough way of saying "older sister." I chose to translate it as "Sis".

-onee-san: it is a polite way to refer to an older sister or an older woman you know. I chose to translate it as  "Big Sister" or "Miss", depending on the context.

-nee-chan: it is an affectionate way to refer to an older sister. I chose to translate is as "Big Sis".

-senpai: left untranslated when used as a form of address. When referring to a group of people or describing the senpai/kohai relationship, it is translated as "senior(s)" (with "junior(s)" used for kohai).

Other honorifics were left unchanged.

**Other notes:**

1) In later chapters, they introduce the term 賢者 (kenja), which basically means "sage." In this case, however, it's used jokingly to refer to someone practicing sexual abstinence, so I decided that "monk" fits the joke better.

**Technical details**

The patch I provided is only compatible with the Windows Steam release of the game.

The backup files are for the Japanese version. There's no real reason to use it over the Chinese one; in fact, you'd miss out on Shun's additional story and CG. 

Most of the UI and images remain untranslated because I don’t have the skills necessary to edit them. However, they’re fairly intuitive, so I don’t think this will be a major issue.  To be honest, even if could edit the images, it wouldn't look great.

**Bonus**

I included my text-only translations of the fan book comics in the bonus PDF file. The bonus file also contains an ending guide, menu translations and a very old popularity poll conducted by Tetrascope. It includes some fun character dialogue, and the winner even received a pretty CG.

**Name options**

Because name input is limited to five characters, the MC’s name can’t really be represented properly using English characters in the standard version. If you want to play as "Nozomi", I made a separate patch called Nozomi ver., which fixes her name accordingly.

Her name isn’t voiced regardless of input, but she is referred to as Nozomi in some of the extra content and in the manga included with the fan book.
